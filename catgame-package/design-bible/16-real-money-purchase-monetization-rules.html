16. Real-Money Purchase / Monetization Rules

Full purchasable scope confirmed — only Booster Pack contents/pricing remain (deferred)
Checkbox clicks won’t be saved to disk until you connect the file.

§ What's locked

The boundaries of monetization need to be defined before any commercial assets are created.

  • ✓ Gems are the premium currency — primarily purchased with real money, but a small amount can also be earned through in-game bonuses and event-related rewards (revised from an earlier "no free-to-play earn path" decision). Gems can be spent to buy Fel (the persistent soft currency) directly.
  • ✓ Properties are purchased with Gems — this is the "real-money purchase" mechanism referenced in Section 09, mediated through the premium currency rather than a direct real-money transaction.
  • ✓ Booster Packs are sold in the in-game Market, purchasable with Gems. (Contents/rarity odds not yet defined — connects to Section 17, Card Rarity & Collection System.)
  • ✓ Properties remain a real-money purchase (locked in Section 09), exclusive to Prized Games (Section 22).
  • ✓ Property leveling costs Fel, not Gems directly — but since Gems buy Fel, real money still indirectly accelerates property progression. See Section 11 for the cost table.
Direction already implied The equipment system already points toward a strong answer: purchased equipment should not automatically translate into permanent combat superiority.

§ Open questions

  • Additional real-money purchases: Yes — cosmetic cats, board themes, and other cosmetics are all purchasable, alongside properties/Gems/Booster Packs.
  • Booster Pack contents and Gem price — how many cards, what rarity odds, how much does a pack cost?
