12. Property Card Design

Coin/equipment/HP/movement effects confirmed possible — 10 open questions remain
Checkbox clicks won’t be saved to disk until you connect the file.

§ What's locked

Card mechanics need to be established before any property deck is illustrated. Several effect types are now confirmed possible, even though the deck's full contents aren't designed yet.

  • ✓ Property cards can grant Coins (amount depends on the specific card drawn) — locked in Section 10.
  • ✓ Property cards can grant Prize Cards — 3 total exist per game instance, seeded into eligible property decks at game start — locked in Section 14/15.
  • ✓ Property cards can grant equipment levels, as one of several possible effects — locked in Section 07/08.
  • ✓ Property cards can also inflict negative status effects: Poison (1 HP loss per tile moved) and Paralysis (movement capped at 2 for 5 turns) — both curable only by visiting the Church (Section 02/04).
  • ✓ This answers "whether cards modify equipment," "whether cards modify coins," "whether cards modify HP," and "whether cards modify movement" from the list below — all: yes.
Worked example — Fish Market Sample cards: Salmon, Tuna, Purchase Discount, Fresh Catch, Spoiled Fish, Fisherman's Luck.

§ Open questions

  • Whether cards modify HP: Yes — negative cards can inflict Poison (1 HP loss per tile moved, cured only by the Church).
  • Whether cards modify movement: Yes — negative cards can inflict Paralysis (movement capped at 2 tiles for 5 turns, curable early by the Church).
  • Whether cards modify PvP

  • Whether the owner controls card selection
