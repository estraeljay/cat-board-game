§ What's locked

The single most important system in the design, now fully resolved. Every other system's kickoff decisions can take their cues from the shape of a single turn.

  • ✓ Turn sequence: Start Turn → Roll → Move full distance rolled → Resolve Landing Location → Check Proximity → Optional PvP → End Turn.
  • ✓ Cards can only be used after landing, on the active player's own turn — never before rolling, and never reactively during another player's turn.
  • ✓ Three separate card pools: Property Deck (drawn only by a visitor who stops on a property tile — the owner does not draw), Equipment Set (the player's own equipment cards), and PvP Deck (usable only inside a PvP battle).
  • ✓ Passing through a property without stopping triggers nothing. (Poison and Paralysis — see Section 04/12 — are visit-triggered property card effects, requiring a full stop; they don't apply on pass-through. A separate pass-through-specific mechanic remains hypothetical/undefined.)
  • ✓ Proximity check: after landing, the active player checks all squares at distance 0–1 (orthogonal + diagonal, i.e. king-move adjacency, including the landed-on tile itself) for other players. For each player found, the active player gets a 3-option menu: Initiate PvP, Do Nothing, or Propose Alliance.
  • ✓ Proposing an alliance sends an immediate accept/decline interrupt to the target player (not deferred to their own turn). If declined, the active player may still choose to initiate PvP against that player in the same turn.
  • ✓ Landing on another player's tile does not auto-trigger PvP — it's always a choice from the 3-option menu, even at distance 0.
  • ✓ Multiple players can occupy the same tile with no blocking. If PvP is declined or unavailable, they're simply shown coexisting on that tile.
  • ✓ Win condition: king-of-the-hill style — the game ends when one player, or one allied group of players, is the last remaining on the board.
  • ✓ Turn timer: each normal turn has a 20-second limit. If the active player hasn't acted (rolled) in that time, the turn is auto-skipped entirely — no roll, no move, nothing happens, and play passes to the next player. PvP battles use their own 30-second-per-turn limit instead (revised from an earlier 20-second placeholder) — full detail in Section 05 (PvP Turn Structure).
Amendment — unification pass 2026-08-28 Two mechanics were promoted to canon from the validated prototype (authoritative detail in MASTER-DATA-SHEET.md §2): (1) a conditional 4th proximity option, "Heal" — shown only when the chosen target is Poisoned and a current ally; cures their Poison, ends the turn, free, not class-locked. (2) an opening grace period — during each player's own first 2 turns they can neither act on nor be targeted by the proximity menu. The proximity check also now resolves as: pick one target in range, then see the menu for that target.
Amendment — design revision 2026-08-28 Movement is now roll-a-maximum. The roll sets an upper bound; the active player walks the path one tile at a time — arrow keys / WASD to step, Enter to stop — up to that maximum, so they can land exactly where they want. Authoritative detail in MASTER-DATA-SHEET.md §2. Reason: under "move exactly the rolled number" it was a hassle to reliably land on a specific property / special-location entry tile. Randomness is deliberately kept — you still can't guarantee catching a fleeing player or dodging every encounter — so the chasing / alliance / betrayal tension is preserved. Snared (odd check die) still forces exactly 1 tile.
New system flagged The win condition introduces alliances/teams as a formal mechanic — not previously defined anywhere in the design bible. This needs a dedicated pass when we reach Section 14 (Victory Condition), and will also touch Section 15 (Elimination) and Section 18 (Multiplayer Rules).
Belongs elsewhere when reached Two numbers decided in this session need a home in sections not yet started: maximum 5 players per game (Section 18, Multiplayer Rules) and the 20-second PvP action timer (Section 05, PvP Turn Structure).
Why this came first Board structure, the character system, HP math, and PvP timing all take their cues from the shape of a single turn. Locking this first prevents rework across every other system.

§ Resolved decisions

All 10 questions from this section are answered. Click a checkmark to flag one for revisiting if something downstream changes it.

  • Turn sequence: Start Turn → Roll → Move full rolled distance → Resolve Landing Location → Check Proximity → Optional PvP → End Turn. Each turn has a 20-second limit; if the player hasn't rolled in time, the turn is auto-skipped entirely.
  • Cards before rolling: Not allowed. Cards can only be used after landing.
  • Cards after movement: Yes — this is the only window in which cards can be played.
  • Cards during another player's turn: Not allowed, ever — no reactive plays, not even Guard cards. Reactive PvP timing will need its own resolution in Section 05.
  • PvP trigger: After landing, for each player found within distance 0–1 (8-directional adjacency, including the landed-on tile), the active player gets a 3-option menu: Initiate PvP, Do Nothing, or Propose Alliance. Alliance proposals interrupt the target player immediately for accept/decline; if declined, the active player may still initiate PvP that turn.
  • Auto-initiate on landing: No — the 3-option menu is always a choice, even when landing directly on another player.
  • Choosing a target: Yes — the active player picks exactly one target from all players in range.
  • Passing through a property: No trigger. Poison/Paralysis (Section 04/12) are visit-only card effects, not pass-through effects.
  • Multiple players on one tile: Allowed, no blocking. Falls under the optional PvP rule; if declined, players simply coexist on the tile.
  • Win condition: King-of-the-hill — last remaining player, or last remaining allied group, wins. (Introduces a new alliance/team mechanic — see note above. Full detail deferred to Section 14.)
