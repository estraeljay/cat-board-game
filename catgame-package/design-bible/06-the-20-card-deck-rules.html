6. The 20-Card Deck Rules

All questions resolved
Checkbox clicks won’t be saved to disk until you connect the file.

§ What's locked

Established so far.

  • Deck is 10 Attack + 10 Guard cards.
  • One use per card per deck cycle.
  • ✓ Starting hand size: 5 cards, drawn at the start of each PvP battle (locked in Section 05).
  • ✓ Draw frequency: 1 card per own turn, during the Draw Phase — confirms players draw every turn.
UI impact These answers will have a major impact on the hand, attack-table, and deck-state UI screens (see Section 20).

§ Resolved decisions

  • Maximum hand size: A cap exists (proposed ~10) — excess cards are discarded on draw if over the limit.
  • Voluntary discard: No — cards only leave the hand through normal Attack/Guard play, not player choice.
  • Whether discarded cards become "Used": No — played cards are simply removed from play, no separate tracked state.
  • Whether unused cards can be retained between deck cycles: No — a full cycle (all 20 cards used) resets the deck completely fresh.
  • Whether tabled Attack cards count toward hand/card limits: N/A — an Attack card resolves immediately within the Battle Phase (Section 05), so it never sits tabled long enough to interact with hand limits.
  • Maximum number of simultaneous tabled Attack cards: Always exactly 1 (or 0) — only the active turn's single Attack card is ever tabled, and it resolves before the turn ends. No "Battle Lock"-style accumulation exists.
  • Whether players can inspect their own used cards: No — once used, not inspectable again.
  • Whether opponents can see the tabled cards: Yes — the card currently being played is visible to the opponent (they see what's being played, even though hand contents and hand size stay hidden).
  • Whether opponents can see the number of cards in hand: No — hand size is hidden information.
