Phase 4 — Dungeons Cut from scope

13. Dungeons (cut)

Cut from scope — replaced by the Prize Card reward system (see Section 14)
Checkbox clicks won’t be saved to disk until you connect the file.

§ What's locked

This system has been cut entirely from scope, not resolved. The 15 open questions below are preserved for reference but no longer need answers.

  • ✓ Dungeons do not exist in the game. There is no dungeon system, no dungeon spaces on the board, and no dungeon-sourced rewards.
  • ✓ What dungeons would have provided — a source of rewards and a sink for player time/risk — is instead handled by the Prize Card system: 3 Prize Cards per game instance, seeded into property card decks, which now drive the entire win economy. See Section 14 (Game Victory Condition) and Section 15 (Player Elimination & Persistent Assets) for the full mechanic.
  • ✓ Board Structure (Section 02) confirms there are no dungeon spaces on the 20x20 grid — only the outer path ring, the Town Center, properties, and the Inn/Blacksmith/Church.
Why this was cut Dungeons were an entirely undesigned subsystem (0 of 15 questions answered) sitting on top of an already ambitious scope — turn loop, PvP, alliances/betrayal, property economy, and monetization. Cutting them removed a whole layer of undesigned complexity without losing the core hook: the Prize Card system covers the same "risk vs. reward" role dungeons would have, using mechanics already built elsewhere.
