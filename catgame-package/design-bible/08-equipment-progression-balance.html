8. Equipment Progression Balance

PvP/property interactions locked — 1 open question remains (needs real playtesting)
Checkbox clicks won’t be saved to disk until you connect the file.

§ What's locked

One of the game's core anti-pay-to-win mechanisms, so it deserves its own design pass. Now resolved by the Blacksmith-driven leveling system locked in Section 07.

  • Progression shape established as a template: Level 1 → 2 requires X actions, Level 2 → 3 requires Y actions, etc.
  • ✓ Leveling actions: Blacksmith visits (primary — levels all 8 slots at once) and property card draws (secondary, one of several possible card effects).
  • ✓ Properties can accelerate progression: Yes, via property card draws.
  • ✓ How frequently equipment can be affected: Governed by visit frequency to Blacksmith and properties — not a separately throttled system on top of that.
Before final assets Build a master equipment progression table before producing the final equipment card assets.

§ Open questions

  • Can PvP wins accelerate equipment progression? No — strictly Blacksmith and property cards.
  • Can losing PvP reduce equipment level? No — level never decreases from combat, only resets between matches (this also resolves Section 07's matching question).
  • Can properties damage equipment? Yes — negative property card effects can reduce equipment level, not just accelerate it.
  • How quickly can a player realistically reach maximum level within one match? Depends on Blacksmith visit frequency shared among up to 5 players — needs real playtesting.
