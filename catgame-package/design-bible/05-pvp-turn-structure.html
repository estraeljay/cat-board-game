5. PvP Turn Structure

Deck depletion resolved — only the deferred multi-Guard question remains
Checkbox clicks won’t be saved to disk until you connect the file.

§ What's locked

Full multi-round turn structure, modeled on a Draw/Battle/End phase sequence. Simplified from an earlier Spell/Trap concept down to just Attack and Guard cards, matching the existing 20-card deck.

  • ✓ Who goes first: the player who initiated the PvP battle (via the proximity menu in Section 01) attacks first.
  • ✓ Battle setup: both players draw a 5-card starting hand at the start of the PvP battle.
  • ✓ Turn timer: 30 seconds per turn. If unused, the turn automatically ends (superseding an earlier placeholder mention of a 20-second PvP timer in Sections 01/14 — those should be updated to 30 seconds for consistency).
  • ✓ Turn phases, in order:
    1. Draw Phase — the active player draws 1 card.
    2. Battle Phase — the active player sets one Attack card face-up as their attack. The opponent may respond by activating one Guard card from hand to defend (or choose not to).
    3. End Phase — the turn ends and passes to the opponent, who becomes the new active player.
  • ✓ Roles alternate: the active player is always the attacker for their own turn; the other player is the defender who may react with a Guard card. These roles swap each turn.
  • ✓ Cards played per turn: exactly one Attack card on your own turn (as attacker); up to one Guard card as a reaction when defending on the opponent's turn. Not both in the same turn — Attack and Guard belong to different roles within a round-trip, not stacked on one turn.
  • ✓ Guard cards are therefore always played outside your own turn — specifically during the opponent's Battle Phase, as a reaction to their Attack.
  • ✓ An Attack card resolves immediately within the Battle Phase, after the defender has had the chance to respond with a Guard card (or pass).
  • ✓ "Battle Lock" is dropped — an undefined term from earlier design notes, superseded by this phase structure.
  • ✓ No stacking effects — with Spell/Trap cards dropped, only Attack and Guard cards exist, each with a single printed value. There's nothing to stack.
  • ✓ Battle ends at 0 HP or an accepted withdrawal (proposed by either player, any point mid-battle, one attempt per battle — locked in Section 14).

§ Resolved decisions

  • Who gets the first turn: The PvP initiator.
  • What a player can do on their turn: Draw Phase → Battle Phase (set an Attack card) → End Phase.
  • How many cards can they play: One Attack card on their own turn; one Guard card as a reaction when defending.
  • One Attack + one Guard in the same turn: No — they belong to different roles (attacker vs. defender), not combined on one turn.
  • Guard cards outside the player's turn: Yes — always played reactively during the opponent's Battle Phase.
  • When an Attack card resolves: Immediately in the Battle Phase, after the defender's chance to Guard.
  • Battle Lock: Dropped — superseded by this phase structure.
  • Effect stacking: N/A — no Spell/Trap cards, nothing to stack.
  • When a player draws: 5-card starting hand at battle start, then 1 card per own turn during the Draw Phase.

§ Open questions

  • Deck empties mid-battle: Standard shuffle-when-empty — discard pile reshuffles back into the deck, same pattern as property decks (Section 09).

§ Open question (deferred to asset creation)

  • Can a player react to one incoming Attack with more than one Guard card, or is it strictly one-for-one? (Same question as Section 04 — decided when card assets are built.)
