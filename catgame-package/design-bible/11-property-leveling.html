11. Property Leveling

Level 5 confirmed as hard cap — only Coin thresholds remain (deferred, needs rent/card values first)
Checkbox clicks won’t be saved to disk until you connect the file.

§ What's locked

Revised again: the visit-count gate is dropped entirely in favor of a more organic metric — cumulative Coins the property itself has earned. Fel remains the cost.

  • ✓ Visits dropped as the gate — replaced by cumulative Coins earned by the property (from rent + property card draws), tracked across every match it's been placed in, same cross-game cumulative logic the visit count used to have.
  • ✓ Fel cost stays fixed at the original numbers (750/1,750/3,500/6,000), unaffected by variable stakes (Section 22). Fel is persistent and fungible — it doesn't matter which stake tier it was earned from, so no rebalancing is needed here. Only the Coin threshold column above still needs numbers, and that's for an unrelated reason (rent rates/property card values aren't designed yet).
  • ✓ Both the Coin threshold and the Fel cost must be satisfied to level up.
  • ✓ Property leveling is intentionally tied to Fel (which Gems can buy) rather than being fully disconnected from monetization — see Section 16.
Needs a full rebalance pass The old visit thresholds (15/35/65/110) don't translate directly into Coin amounts — a property's Coin income depends on rent rate and card-draw frequency, neither of which has an exact number yet. This whole table's left column needs real numbers once rent amounts and property card effects are designed, plus playtesting to validate the pacing.

§ Open questions

  • Actual Coin thresholds per level — depends on rent rate and property card Coin rewards, neither designed yet.
  • Level cap: Level 5 is a hard cap — no levels beyond it.
