4. HP and Combat Mathematics

Core formula locked — 3 small open questions remain (crits, damage cap, multi-Guard)
Checkbox clicks won’t be saved to disk until you connect the file.

§ What's locked

Combat math is now defined around a single formula with a guaranteed-progress floor, built for a multi-round card battle (see Section 05 for the full turn structure).

  • 0 HP = eliminated from the entire board-game instance.
  • ✓ Base HP is 100, modified only by equipment — there is no separate character/cat level system. Equipment slot → stat mapping: Weapon, Gloves, Ring → Attack; Shield, Body Armor, Boots → Defense; Helmet, Necklace → HP. All 8 slots are combat-relevant (revised — Boots was originally movement-flavored/non-combat, now folded into Defense). Exact per-level magnitude is deferred to when the actual equipment cards are designed.
  • ✓ Damage formula: Damage = max(1, (Attacker's Attack stat + Attack card bonus) − Defender's Defense stat − Guard card block value). The max(1, ...) floor guarantees at least 1 damage always gets through on a resolved attack — this is what makes "neither player can meaningfully damage the other" mathematically impossible, resolving that open question directly.
  • ✓ No counterattacks, no simultaneous damage: only the attacking player (per their turn — see Section 05) deals damage in an exchange. A Guard card purely blocks; it never deals damage back. Roles (attacker/defender) alternate turn-by-turn instead of both players attacking at once.
  • ✓ Turn order: the player who initiated the PvP battle (via the proximity menu in Section 01) goes first as attacker. Turns then alternate between the two players — see Section 05 for the full phase structure.
  • ✓ Battle duration: multi-round, no hard cap on number of rounds. A battle ends when a player reaches 0 HP, or when a proposed withdrawal is accepted (either player may propose withdrawal at any point mid-battle — only one attempt allowed per battle, per Section 14).
  • ✓ Healing: the Inn (one of the 3 special board locations from Section 02) heals HP for a Coin cost (amount deferred alongside other numeric balance items).
  • ✓ Poison status effect: deals 1 HP damage per tile moved while active (a 5-tile move costs 5 HP). Inflicted by negative property card effects (Section 12). Persists until cured — no stated natural expiration, only removable by visiting the Church (Section 02), for a Coin cost.
  • ✓ Paralysis status effect: caps movement to 2 tiles regardless of dice roll, lasting 5 turns. Inflicted by negative property card effects (Section 12). Expires naturally after 5 turns, or early via the Church for a Coin cost.
  • ✓ Armor/Shield interaction: resolved through the general Defense stat in the formula above — no separate special-case mechanic beyond that (e.g. no "shield blocks one full hit").

Poison now also comes from Poison Swamp board tiles (Section 02), not only property cards, and can now be cured either at the Church (Coin cost) or by an allied player's free Heal proximity action (Section 01). The per-tile bleed is the entire effect — there is no separate "hit on entry." Unrelated: the cat class roster is locked at exactly 3 (Knight/Mage/Priest); the 3 per-class special abilities are still undesigned.
Still open Three things remain undecided: whether critical hits exist at all, whether there's a damage maximum/cap (a minimum floor of 1 is locked, but no ceiling), and how a single Guard card interacts if an opponent's Attack could theoretically be countered more than once (current assumption: one Guard card per incoming Attack, no stacking — but this wasn't explicitly confirmed).

§ Resolved decisions

  • Starting/Maximum HP: Base 100, modified by equipped gear (no cap defined beyond whatever total equipment can add).
  • Attack calculation: Attack stat (from Weapon/Gloves/Ring) + Attack card's printed bonus.
  • Defense calculation: Defense stat (from Shield/Body Armor) + Guard card's printed block value, both subtracted from incoming damage.
  • Equipment contribution: See the slot → stat mapping above.
  • Attack card contribution: A printed bonus value added to the Attack stat.
  • Guard card contribution: A printed flat block value subtracted from incoming damage.
  • Damage minimum: 1 (guaranteed floor, see formula above). No maximum defined yet.
  • Healing: The Inn special location heals HP (amount TBD).
  • Damage reduction / Armor / Shield interaction: All handled through the Defense stat in the general formula — no separate mechanic.
  • Counterattacks: None — Guard purely blocks, never deals damage back.
  • Simultaneous damage: Doesn't occur — only the active attacker of a given turn deals damage; roles alternate instead.
  • Turn order: PvP initiator attacks first, then turns alternate.
  • Battle duration: Multi-round, no hard cap — ends at 0 HP or an accepted withdrawal.
  • What happens if neither player can meaningfully damage the other: Can't happen — the damage floor of 1 guarantees progress every resolved attack.

§ Open questions (deferred to asset creation)

Not blocking further design work — these get decided when the actual card/equipment assets are built, same treatment as the equipment level-up magnitudes.

  • Do critical hits exist at all?
  • Is there a damage maximum/cap, or is damage uncapped above the floor of 1?
  • Can more than one Guard card be played against a single incoming Attack, or is it strictly one-for-one?
