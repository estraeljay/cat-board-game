7. Equipment System

Nearly fully resolved — 3 open questions remain, all deferred rewards/assets
Checkbox clicks won’t be saved to disk until you connect the file.

§ What's locked

"Temporary match-level power" is now a concrete system: every equipped item resets to Level 1 at the start of each match and levels back up through play, primarily via the Blacksmith.

  • Eight equipment slots: Ring, Necklace, Boots, Gloves, Body Armor, Helmet, Weapon, Shield.
  • Core principle: permanent ownership, temporary match-level power.
  • ✓ Stat mapping (locked in Section 04): Weapon, Gloves, Ring → Attack; Shield, Body Armor, Boots → Defense; Helmet, Necklace → HP.
  • ✓ Starting level: Always Level 1 at the start of every match — fully fresh each game, no carryover advantage from the item's permanent rarity/quality.
  • ✓ Slots level independently — each of the 8 slots tracks its own level number.
  • ✓ Leveling sources: primarily Blacksmith (a special board location, Section 02) — one visit, for a Coin cost, levels up all 8 slots by +1 at once (proposed default, open to adjustment). Property card draws can also grant equipment levels as one of several possible effects (alongside Coins and Prize Cards).
  • ✓ Maximum level per match: proposed 3-5, sized to be realistically reachable within a single ~20-40 min match given Blacksmith is one shared location among up to 5 players (needs real playtesting to confirm the exact ceiling).
  • ✓ Exact Attack/Defense/HP contribution per level is deferred to when the actual equipment card assets are designed (same treatment as combat card values in Section 04).
Proposed, not fully confirmed Two specific numbers above (all 8 slots leveling together per Blacksmith visit, and the 3-5 max level range) are reasonable defaults rather than firm decisions — flag if you want them changed.

§ Resolved decisions

  • Starting equipment level: Always Level 1 at match start.
  • Maximum launch level: Proposed 3-5 per match (needs playtesting).
  • How each equipment type levels up: Primarily Blacksmith visits, plus property card draws as a secondary source.
  • Equipment XP vs. visit/action milestones: Visit-based (Blacksmith visits), not a separate XP pool.
  • Whether equipment levels independently: Yes, each of the 8 slots tracks its own level.
  • Attack/Defense contribution per level: Mapping locked (Section 04); exact magnitude deferred to asset creation.

§ Resolved decisions

  • Equipment acquisition: Multiple paths — Booster Packs (Gems) plus other sources like Campaign rewards and events.
  • Equipment swapping: Yes, between matches — players choose their loadout from owned equipment before each match starts, then it's locked for that match (same pattern as property placement).
  • Whether equipment can be changed during a match: No — loadout is locked once a match starts, same treatment as property placement.
  • Whether equipment can be traded: Yes, similar to properties.
  • Whether equipment can be sold: Yes, similar to properties.
  • Whether equipment has durability: No — doesn't degrade or break; only the temporary match-level resets each game.
  • How property effects interact with maximum level: Bounded by it — both positive and negative property effects are capped at the match's max level (3-5), never exceeding it.
  • Floor at Level 1: Hard floor — negative property card effects cannot push equipment level below 1.
