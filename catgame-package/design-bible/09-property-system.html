9. Property System

Nearly fully resolved — 1 open question remains (exact Gem price, deferred)
Checkbox clicks won’t be saved to disk until you connect the file.

§ What's locked

One of the most developed systems, but important pieces are still missing.

  • Permanent property ownership.
  • Real-money purchase — specifically via Gems, the premium currency (see Section 16).
  • Owner can place a property on a blank board area before a game begins.
  • Other players can interact with it.
  • Property has its own 10–20 card effect deck.
  • Property cards trigger when players stop on / enter it — current visit definition is a player ending their dice movement on it.
  • Property levels based on visits — superseded, see below.
  • Level-up requires in-game coins — refined to Fel, see below.
  • Property can eventually be sold through an in-game market.
  • ✓ Properties exist only in Prized Games — Campaign mode (Section 22) has no properties on its board at all.
  • ✓ Leveling gate revised: instead of a raw visit count, a property's progress toward its next level is now driven by cumulative Coins the property itself has earned (from rent payments and property card draws) across every match it's been placed in. Fel is still the cost paid to actually execute the level-up once the Coin threshold is met. Exact Coin thresholds need a full rebalancing pass — see Section 11.
  • ✓ Rent: visitors pay the owner directly on visit — proposed in Coins (flagged, not fully confirmed; could be Fel instead). This is both the direct owner benefit and now the source that feeds property leveling.
  • ✓ Level effect on payout: rent stays flat regardless of property level — instead, higher levels improve the quality/rarity of drawn cards (connects to Section 12, Property Card Design, and Section 17, Card Rarity).
  • ✓ Placement is locked in once chosen for a game instance — no mid-match relocation. Owners still pick a fresh spot each new game (Section 19).

§ Open questions

  • Exact property purchase pricing — currency mechanism resolved (Gems), exact Gem amount still undecided
  • Property ownership rules: Single owner only — no co-ownership, straightforward individual ownership like everything else in the game (proposed default, no contradicting info).
  • Number of properties one player can own: Max 2 per player, decided at game setup (locked in Section 02).
  • Property placement restrictions: Must be a blank interior tile (Section 02), and must be at least 1 tile away from any other property — no two property blocks can sit directly adjacent.
  • Property size: 3x3 block + 1 entry tile, impassable except the entry tile (locked in Section 02).
  • Whether properties can be moved after placement: No — fixed for the entire game instance once placed.
  • Whether property placement is permanent for the game instance: Yes. (Owners still choose a fresh spot each new game, per Section 19 — it just can't change mid-match.)
  • Whether the owner receives benefits when other players visit: Yes — rent, paid in Coins (proposed).
  • Whether visitors pay the owner: Yes, Coins-based rent (proposed).
  • Whether property cards affect the owner: No — card effects only ever apply to the visitor who drew them. The owner's only direct benefit is flat rent (Coins).
  • Property card draw frequency: Exactly 1 card per visit.
  • Property deck reshuffle rules: Standard shuffle-when-empty — discard pile reshuffles back into the deck once exhausted.
  • Property level maximum at launch: Level 5 (per the progression table in Section 11).
  • Property level benefits: Rent stays flat regardless of level — instead, card quality/rarity improves as the property levels up.
  • Property upgrade costs: See Section 11's Fel cost table.
  • Property selling formula: No fixed formula — the market is player-driven (Section 10), so the seller sets their own asking price. A 5% resale fee applies on the transaction.
  • Market fees / taxes: 5% resale fee (locked in Section 10).
  • Whether property can be sold while placed on a board: No — must be unplaced/not in an active game to sell.
  • What happens to the property if its owner is eliminated: Fully resolved in Section 15 — protected, stays on board but inactive, ownership untouched.
